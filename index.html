✨ What is MultiTaskbar?

MultiTaskbar is your essential companion for dual-monitor productivity on Windows 11. If you have a second monitor, you know the struggle of constantly moving your mouse to your primary screen just to check the time, switch apps, or adjust volume. MultiTaskbar puts an end to that.

This tool gives you a fully functional taskbar on your second monitor — and yes, it works on every extra screen you have! You get a familiar Windows taskbar experience, but now on every display. It's completely free and open-source, so you can use it with confidence.

🔧 How MultiTaskbar Works

You might be wondering, "Why doesn't Windows just do this?" Great question! While Windows 11 has improved multi-monitor support, it still doesn't give you a full taskbar on every screen by default. That's where MultiTaskbar steps in.

MultiTaskbar works by detecting all your connected displays and placing a separate taskbar on each one. It stays in sync with your primary taskbar, showing the same open windows, pinned apps, and system tray information. The difference is that you can access everything right from your second screen — no more reaching over!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🤔 Is MultiTaskbar safe to use?

Absolutely! It's open-source, which means the entire codebase is publicly available for anyone to review. No data collection, no telemetry, no ads. Just a clean utility that does exactly what it claims.

⚙️ Will it interfere with games?

Not at all. MultiTaskbar runs as a lightweight utility that respects your active window. In exclusive fullscreen games, it won't show over game content — it'll be waiting for you when you tab out.

🔁 How do I restart MultiTaskbar?

Simply close the app and open it again — same as any Windows program. It saves your preferences automatically, so your setup stays the same. You can also set it to start with Windows for convenience.
